How long does it take to receive my loan?

Once the processing fee is confirmed on the blockchain, your full loan amount will be processed and disbursed within 24 hours. In most cases, clients receive their funds much sooner.

Is my information secure?

Absolutely. PrimeLend uses state-of-the-art encryption and security protocols to protect your personal and financial information. Your privacy and security are our top priorities.

What cryptocurrencies do you accept for the processing fee?

We currently accept Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and other major cryptocurrencies. Please contact our support team for specific wallet addresses and instructions.

What happens if I can't pay the processing fee?

The processing fee is required to initiate the loan process. If you're unable to pay the fee, we recommend contacting our support team to discuss your options.

Can I repay my loan early?

Yes, PrimeLend encourages early repayment. There are no penalties for paying off your loan ahead of schedule. We believe in flexibility and supporting our clients' financial well-being.
